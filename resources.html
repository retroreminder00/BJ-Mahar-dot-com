GEOLOGY, MINES & OTHER FUN STUFF

What's under my house?

Colorado has mines, unusual geology, expansive soils and a long history underground. These tools are great for research, but they're not a substitute for a geotechnical engineer or other qualified professional when a property actually has a concern.

Colorado Geological Survey GIS Portal → Historic Mining Districts → Abandoned Mine Lands → Colorado Coal Mines & Coal Fields →

Historical mine and geology datasets vary in age, completeness and accuracy. Use them as research tools, not as engineering or safety determinations.
